GreenLens: see your crops through AI

GreenLens is an AI-powered crop recognition system. Take a photo in the field, and our custom-trained computer vision model tells you which crops it sees, how many, and how confident it is.

Try GreenLens How it works
How it works

From field photo to insight in seconds

Sign in

Create a free account on the mobile app or website. One account works on both.

Upload a photo

Take a picture with your phone camera or upload an image from your gallery or computer.

AI detection

Our YOLO-based deep learning model finds and labels each crop in the image.

Review results

See the labelled image with the count and confidence for each crop, saved to your history.

Supported crops

Trained on India's key field crops

GreenLens currently recognises five major crops, and we will keep adding more.

🌾Rice
🌾Wheat
🌽Maize
🎋Sugarcane
🌿Jute
Features

Built for the field and the office

Smart detection

Each crop found in the image is outlined and labelled by a custom-trained model.

Counts & confidence

A clear summary of each crop detected, with how many were found and the model's confidence.

Detection history

Every scan is saved with its original and annotated image, so you can look back anytime.

Mobile app

Android and iOS app made for use in the field, straight from your phone camera.

Web platform

Run detections and manage your history from any browser.

Synced & secure

Your account and results are stored in the cloud and stay in sync across mobile and web.
