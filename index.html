Our vision

Technology that helps farmers farm better

We believe technology can play a meaningful role in how farming decisions are made. Through AI-driven solutions, we aim to give farmers clear, timely insight into the conditions that shape their crops and productivity.

We are committed to building practical, intelligent and farmer-focused products — tools that help improve farming practices, increase efficiency and adapt to changing agricultural conditions.

When we bring the power of technology closer to the farmer, we help create a better future for agriculture.

Our first product

GreenLens

GreenLens is an AI-powered crop recognition system. Upload a photo from the field and our custom-trained computer vision model identifies the crops in it — showing what was found, how many, and how confident the model is.

Currently trained to recognise:

RiceWheatMaizeSugarcaneJute
  1. 1Capture or upload a crop image from your phone or browser.
  2. 2Our YOLO-based deep learning model analyses the image in seconds.
  3. 3See labelled results with counts and confidence, saved to your history.
