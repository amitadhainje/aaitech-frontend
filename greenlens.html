How It Works

From Field Photo to Insight

No setup, no expertise required. Just upload and see.

01

Upload a Photo

Take a picture with your phone or upload one from your gallery or computer.

02

AI Detection

Our YOLO-based deep learning model finds and labels each crop in the image.

03

Review Results

Features

Built for the Field and the Office

Smart Detection

Each crop found in the image is outlined and labelled by our custom-trained model.

Counts & Confidence

A clear summary of each crop detected, with how many were found and the model's confidence.

Detection History

Every scan is saved with its original and annotated image, so you can look back anytime.

Mobile App

Android and iOS app made for use in the field, straight from your phone camera.

Web Platform

Run detections and manage your history from any browser.

Synced & Secure

One account for mobile and web, with results stored securely in the cloud.
