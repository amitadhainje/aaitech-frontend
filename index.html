Our Vision

Technology that helps farmers farm better

We believe technology can play a meaningful role in helping farmers make better decisions. Through AI-driven solutions, we aim to give farmers valuable insight into climatic conditions, soil health, weather patterns and the other factors that directly impact their crops and productivity.

We are committed to building practical, intelligent and farmer-focused products that help improve farming practices, increase efficiency and adapt to changing agricultural conditions.

When we bring the power of technology closer to the farmer, we can help create a better future for agriculture.

Climate & Weather

Insight into climatic conditions and weather patterns across the crop cycle.

Soil Health

Understanding soil conditions for healthier, more productive land.

Crop Intelligence

Computer vision that recognises crops directly from field photos.

Informed Decisions

Turning data into practical guidance farmers can act on.

Our Product

GreenLens

Our AI-powered crop detection app for mobile and web. Take a photo in the field and GreenLens identifies the crops in it, with counts and confidence for each.

Mobile App

Android and iOS app built for use right in the field, straight from the phone camera.

Crop Detection

Recognises rice, wheat, maize, sugarcane and jute using our custom-trained model.

Web Platform

Run detections and review your history from any browser, synced with the app.
